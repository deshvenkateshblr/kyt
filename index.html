Your trip is a list of steps, and every step is yours to shape: what it is, when, and where. Add a Google Maps link, a note, a ticket or a PDF. Move steps around when the day changes. On the day, KYT shows the step you're on.

  • Reorder steps whenever plans change
  • Keep each ticket or PDF with the step it belongs to

Add KYT to your home screen.

There is no app store and nothing to download. KYT opens full screen, like any app on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Safari browser
  1. 1 Tap the Share button in Safari's bottom toolbar.
  2. 2 Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3 Tap Add in the top right corner.

Already using KYT in Safari? The Home Screen app keeps its own storage and starts empty. Before adding it, tap Back up or restore your data in the menu above and download a backup. Then open the Home Screen app and restore it there.

Android

Chrome browser
  1. 1 Tap the More menu ⋮ in Chrome.
  2. 2 Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. 3 Tap Install or Add to place KYT on your screen.
Offline-ready before you take off: Open KYT once with an active internet connection so all trip data and maps cache locally. After that, it functions seamlessly mid-flight and in remote areas without cellular data.
